# RevOps - Revenue Operations

Revenue Operations workflows for sales, marketing, and customer success automation.

## Workflows

### Sales (5)
| Workflow | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Lead Capture and CRM Sync | Multi-source lead capture with AI enrichment and CRM sync |
| Sales Follow-up Sequences | Automated follow-up cadences with AI personalization |
| Quote and Proposal Generation | AI-generated quotes and professional proposals |
| Deal Alerts and Pipeline Management | Deal stage tracking and pipeline alerts |
| Sales Reporting Dashboard | Weekly/monthly sales analytics and reporting |

### Marketing (4)
| Workflow | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Social Media Scheduling and Publishing | Multi-platform content scheduling and publishing |
| Email Campaign Management | Campaign automation with A/B testing |
| Marketing Analytics and Reporting | Performance tracking with AI insights |
| Content Distribution and Syndication | Cross-platform content distribution |

### Customer Success (3)
| Workflow | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Customer Success and Retention | Health scores, NPS, churn prediction, renewals |
| Customer Support Ticketing | Multi-channel support with AI triage and CSAT |
| Voice AI Receptionist | Industry-agnostic AI receptionist for any business |

## Key Features
- AI-powered lead scoring and qualification
- Automated follow-up sequences
- Customer health monitoring
- Churn prediction and prevention
- Multi-channel support integration
- Revenue analytics and forecasting
